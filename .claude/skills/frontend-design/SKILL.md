# Kids-Store Frontend Design Standards

## Colors (Soft Pastels)
- Primary: Warm Coral `#FF8C61` — buttons, CTAs, highlights.
- Secondary: Sky Blue/Teal `#64C9CF` / `#4ECDC4` — tags, badges, secondary highlights.
- Supportive: Sunny Yellow `#FFE17D` — stars, discounts, celebratory icons.
- Background: Warm Cream `#FDFBF7` — cozy, non-sterile base.
- Cards: White `#FFFFFF` with soft shadow `0 10px 30px rgba(0,0,0,0.02)`.

## Typography
- Arabic font: **Tajawal** or **Cairo**; English font: **Outfit** or **Quicksand**.
- Sizes: hero 36-48px, section headers 24-28px, card titles 16-18px, body 14-15px (color `#4A4A4A`).

## Spacing & Borders
- Corner radii: cards 20-24px, buttons/badges 12px or pill (50px), inputs 12-16px.
- Card padding: 16-20px; detail sections: 24-32px; vertical section gap: 64-96px.

## Micro-Interactions
- All transitions: `all 0.3s cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)`.
- Card hover: `translateY(-6px)` with increased shadow; button click: `scale(0.96)`.
- Offer badges: bouncy hover `rotate(-2deg)`; icons: soft line-drawing SVGs with warm backgrounds.
